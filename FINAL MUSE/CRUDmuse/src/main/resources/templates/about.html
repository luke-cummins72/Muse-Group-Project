About Muse

Learn the story of Muse and meet the Creators

What is Muse?

Muse is much more than a name, it is the sacred spark that ignites the passion in our souls, urging us to create, to dream, to transform the mundane into something extraordinary. Here at Muse we stride to ignite that passion in all our users souls, whether it be students who want to share their passion with the world or employers who want to discover and give these souls a path to success, Muse offers a place of shared experiences.


We Are Each Other's Muses, Every person holds the potential to be someone else's Muse, a catalyst for creativity, growth, and change. Muse celebrates the idea that inspiration is not a solitary pursuit but a shared journey, where each encounter leaves a trace, and every connection fuels the spark of something greater.


Be part of Muse, where your unique vision can inspire others, and together we can shape a future driven by creativity.

The Creators

Muse consists of three creators, Ailis, Luke and Tia, each one bringing a different and unique skill set to the website. Ailis is our main website coder/programmer every web page you see throughout this website was built and programmed by our very own Ailis Baldwin.


As for the overall design and aesthetic of the website, this was imagined and frame-worked by Luke, our head designer, spanning over ten days Luke designed and laid out each web page individually so Ailis our head of programming had a in dept understanding of the desired look and feel for the final website.


Tia our head of database control built and designed a specific database for this website, her deep understanding of databases and networks allowed us as a team to create a fully functional database that could hold hundreds of students projects and users details.
